The sacredness of voice

In the Section Listen you will find bookable time slots, during which your Khalili language support partners will read aloud for you texts and patterns presented in the learning material. When you interact with our Khalili Team Members you feel that they sense certain sacredness in the voice. Thus, apart from some individual words or short exercises, Eed-b-Eed will refrain from producing recordings, and so Eed-b-Eed Learners will be kindly requested to refrain from recording Listen sessions. This will be of particular importance to our female support partners.

Our approach to using recordings in language teaching has also a very modern dimension. By not posting recordings online, we are preventing Artificial Intelligence companies from unethical “harvesting” these linguistic “data” without properly acknowledging and compensating their authors. As a Learner you will play a vital role in protecting the voice of our partners from misuse.
