Verb Trainer

Discover the joys of juggling Arabic words that describe actions (verbs). How do you say “I wrote it”, “Speak!” or “I didn’t say it”? Eed-b-Eed Beginners are provided with clear visual clues on how to pronounce the words, and more advanced Learners can challenge themselves with jumping across Khalili Arabic and Classical Arabic, to even switching off key pronunciation clues.

You can currently play with a selection of examples. All 2,200 forms that our Khalili Team Members have recently prepared will be available soon. That’s enough practice for more than one afternoon!
