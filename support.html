Eed-b-Eed?
Donate

You may wish to support the development of Eed-b-Eed through a one-off donation or regular giving via appropriate fundraising channels of the University of Aberdeen. Contributions can be made via the dedicated JustGiving page of the Development Trust SCIO, the fundraising body of the University of Aberdeen. Regardless of size, each contribution matters. Click here to donate.

If you are considering a major contribution, please contact Dr Jakub Zbrzezny at jakub.zbrzezny@abdn.ac.uk or +44 1224 272385. He will be pleased to connect you with the appropriate University of Aberdeen administrative colleagues, who can facilitate the process and ensure that any contribution is handled with due diligence and in full compliance with applicable procedures and legal requirements.

Our current goal is to raise approximately £25,000 in total, £5,000 for the work of each of our five Khalili partners. This will allow to engage each partner on a part-time basis for 275 hours, equivalent to two months (nine weeks with six 5h-work days), at our usual hourly rate, corresponding to that of a pre-PhD university research assistant (Grade 5/29: £18.12).

Our team estimates that this will be the minimal time required to create draft didactic materials to cover one year of structured engagement with Khalili Arabic for a regular learner. Once developed, the materials will undergo testing, evaluation, and revision before eventual publication in an open-access academic series.
