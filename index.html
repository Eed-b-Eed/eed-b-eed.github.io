When Learning Arabic Creates Jobs

Whether you want to improve your Arabic, learn it from scratch, or support the development of this initiative, build it Hand-in-Hand (Eed-b-Eed in Arabic) with us!

Among the unsung heroes of Palestinian society are local women. When the world in this troubled Land is falling apart, for some literally and for others metaphorically, these resilient women keep their families going day by day, whatever the circumstances. Their quiet pragmatism is heroic.

To keep your family going, you need income. Where paid work exists, there are areas of Palestine where job opportunities for local women, including those holding university degrees, are very few. This is the case in al-Khalil, the southern West Bank region, known in Hebrew and English as Hebron.

The ancient city of Al-Khalil / Hebron is cherished by Jews, Christians, and Muslims alike for its sacred site associated with the burial place of Abraham, known in Arabic as Ibrahim, a figure of shared reverence among the three traditions. It is also the most populous governorate of Palestine (nearly 885,000 people), and its local Colloquial Arabic, known as Khalili, is the most widely spoken variety of Palestinian Arabic.

This initiative contributes to creating such paid work opportunities for educated Khalili women that are sustainable, legally and financially transparent, as well as apolitical, culturally appropriate, and socially acceptable.

Assisting learners of Arabic in their language learning is such an opportunity, especially when carried out online and part-time, with arrangements that respect both local customs and traditions and the agency of each assistant.
