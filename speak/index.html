Speaking Hand-in-Hand

In the Section Speaking you will be able to arrange individual or small-group coaching sessions with your Khalili language support partners. During the sessions you will actively practise all that you have learnt through other sections.

The most advanced Eed-b-Eed Learners will gradually progress to the live co-reading of Modern Standard Arabic or Classical and Quranic Arabic texts “Hand-in-Hand” with Colloquial Arabic in its Khalili variety. Even if mastery of Arabic is not attainable in the course of a few years of part-time study, in this way, you will come very close to experiencing how a native speaker roams freely across diverse manifestations of Arabic, from Quranic to Colloquial, today and in the past.
